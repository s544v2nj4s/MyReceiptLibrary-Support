# My Receipt Library Support

Support for **My Receipt Library**, an app for scanning, organizing, and reviewing your receipts on iPhone and iPad.

- [Privacy policy](https://s544v2nj4s.github.io/MyReceiptLibrary-Support/privacy.html)
- [Report a bug or ask a question](https://github.com/s544v2nj4s/MyReceiptLibrary-Support/issues/new)

## About the app

My Receipt Library turns paper receipts into a searchable library. Scan a receipt and the app suggests the merchant, date, and total, files it under a category, and adds it to your spending reports. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. Receipts, images, and text recognition never leave your device unless you export a backup yourself.

### Features

- **Scan receipts:** capture a receipt with the camera or choose a photo. Long receipts can be scanned in parts and are joined into one image.
- **Automatic details:** on-device text recognition suggests the merchant, date, and total, skipping tax, tips, cash, and change.
- **Categories:** receipts are categorized from the merchant name. Add your own categories with a name, icon, and color.
- **Merchant library:** remember which category a merchant belongs to, so future receipts from that merchant are categorized automatically.
- **Extra details:** add notes, how you paid, and whether a receipt is a business or tax-deductible expense.
- **Search, filter, and sort:** find receipts by merchant, category, amount, month, or notes, filter by date, and sort by date, amount, merchant, or category. Deleted a receipt by mistake? Tap **Undo**.
- **Spending reports:** see totals by category for the last week, month, or year, and share a report as a spreadsheet (CSV) or PDF.
- **Budgets:** set a monthly budget per category and track this month's spending against it.
- **Currencies:** choose the currency for each receipt (pounds by default).
- **Backup and restore:** export your whole library, including images, to a backup file and restore it later. A weekly automatic backup is also saved in Files.
- **Free and full version:** every feature is free for up to 30 receipts. A one-off Full Version purchase removes the limit. Saved receipts are never locked.

## How to use it

1. **Add a receipt.** Tap the **Scan receipt** button, then scan the receipt with the camera or choose an existing photo. For a long receipt, scan it from top to bottom in several pages.
2. **Check the details.** Detected values are suggestions. Review the merchant, amount, date, and category before saving.
3. **Find receipts.** Use the search bar, the category chips, and the calendar menu in the toolbar to filter by date.
4. **Review your spending.** Open **Reports** to see totals by category, tap a category to see its merchants, and share the report as CSV or PDF.
5. **Keep a backup.** Choose **Database backup** in the **…** menu to export your library regularly.

## FAQ

**Where are my receipts stored?**
On your device only. The app doesn't upload your receipts, images, or recognized text anywhere.

**Where are automatic backups saved?**
In the Files app, under **My Receipt Library › Automatic Backups**. A new backup is saved once a week.

**What happens when I import a backup?**
Importing a backup replaces the current library with the contents of the backup file. Export a backup first if you want to keep what's in the app now.

**What happens when I reach 30 receipts on the free version?**
You can still view, edit, search, report on, and back up every receipt you've saved. To add more receipts, buy the Full Version from the **…** menu. It's a one-off purchase, not a subscription.

**I bought the Full Version on another device. How do I unlock it here?**
Open the Full Version screen from the **…** menu and tap **Restore Purchase**, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**The amount or date was read incorrectly.**
Text recognition works best with a flat, well-lit receipt that fills the frame. You can always correct any detected value before saving, or edit the receipt later.

**Can I add a receipt without the camera?**
Yes. Choose **Choose existing photo** to add a receipt from your photo library.

## Privacy

My Receipt Library doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking, and all text recognition runs on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. See the [privacy policy](https://s544v2nj4s.github.io/MyReceiptLibrary-Support/privacy.html) for details.
