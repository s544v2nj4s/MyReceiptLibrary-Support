My Receipt Library Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

My Receipt Library ("the app") is built so that your receipts stay yours. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • Your receipts, receipt images, and spending data are stored only on your device.
  • We do not have servers that receive or store your data, and the app does not collect any data.

What My Receipt Library Stores, and Where

All of the following is saved locally on your device only, in the app's own storage. It is never transmitted to us or any third party by the app itself:

  • Receipt images you scan or choose from your photos
  • Receipt details such as merchant, date, amount, currency, category, payment method, notes, and business or tax-deductible flags
  • Your merchant library, custom categories, and monthly budgets
  • App preferences such as sort order and whether automatic backup is on

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your personal backup like any other app data — that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Camera and Photos

The app asks for camera access only so you can scan receipts. Photos you choose are picked with the system photo picker, so the app only receives the images you select and does not have access to the rest of your photo library. Images are kept on your device.

Text Recognition

Merchant, date, and total suggestions are read from your receipt images using Apple's on-device Vision framework. This processing happens entirely on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Backups and Exports You Initiate

  • Automatic backups — when turned on, a weekly backup of your library is saved in the app's folder in the Files app (My Receipt Library › Automatic Backups) on your device.
  • Manual backups and reports — when you export a backup file or share a spending report (CSV or PDF), the file goes only where you choose to save or send it. What happens to it after that is up to you and the service you share it with.

The app makes no network requests of its own, other than the App Store purchase flow described below.

Full Version Purchase

My Receipt Library offers an optional, one-off Full Version In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit — the app never receives your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase and billing information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

My Receipt Library does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.
